About

Code-minded.
Hamburg at heart.

I'm Jan-Lukas, usually known online as ItzSnufi. I build software, love Linux, and occasionally turn an innocent "what if?" into a project.

01 The beginning

It started with Minecraft mods.

I started learning Java three years ago because Minecraft mods sounded more fun than just wishing they existed. A lot of persistence later, I got the hang of it. Java has been living in my toolkit ever since.

I still use Java, along with Kotlin for Android apps. Lately, I've been getting more into low-level programming, where the bugs are closer to the hardware. Very considerate of them.

02 Why Linux

Windows felt like too much bloat.

Windows felt like it came with more bloatware than operating system, so I gave Linux a try. I stayed to explore how things work under the hood and to start experiments that occasionally become projects. I'm also interested in information security from a white-hat perspective. And yes, I'm a bit of a Google and Linux fanboy. My browser history has accepted this.

03 How I work

Focused on the tricky bits.

I enjoy getting absorbed in a tricky problem and figuring out what makes it tick. My desk can look chaotic, but I promise there is a system in there. I just can't always find it on the first try.

05 Projects & open source

Building things and sharing the work.

I'm currently working on an operating system for ARM phones. I also build custom systems for anyone who asks, free of charge. The catch is that my calendar, unlike the software, has limited resources.

I'm happy to help out with open-source projects, too. My availability varies, but my willingness is open source as well.

06 Away from code

Badminton and cycling get me away from the screen. Then I get home and start another Linux experiment. Balance is a work in progress.
