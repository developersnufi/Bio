About

Code-minded.
Hamburg at heart.

I'm Jan-Lukas, usually known online as ItzSnufi. I build software, love Linux, and enjoy turning an interesting idea into a real project.

01 The beginning

It started with Minecraft mods.

Years ago, I was playing Minecraft and wanted to make my own mods. That sent me down the Java rabbit hole. I stuck with it, and with a lot of persistence, eventually got the hang of it.

I still use Java today, along with Kotlin for Android apps. Lately, I've also been getting more into low-level programming.

02 Why Linux

Windows felt like too much bloat.

That was what pushed me toward Linux. I stayed for the chance to explore how things work under the hood, and for all the experiments that keep turning into new projects. I'm also interested in information security from a white-hat perspective.

03 How I work

Focused on the tricky bits.

I enjoy getting absorbed in a challenging problem and figuring out what makes it tick. My desk can look a little chaotic; I like to think there's a system in there. Most of the time.

04 Away from code

A change of pace.

Badminton and cycling get me away from the screen. And every now and then, I start another Linux experiment anyway.
